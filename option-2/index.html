06 Common questions

Questions we hear every day

Don't see your question? Call us. The consultation is free.

See all questions →
Q.01What should I do right after an accident?

Report it to your car insurer. See your family doctor. Then call us.

Deadlines apply, so the sooner the better.

Q.02How much does a lawyer cost?

Your first consultation is free. If we take your case, you pay nothing up front.

Our fee is 33% of what we recover, plus HST.OPEN 2

Q.03I was at fault. Can I still claim?

Yes. You can claim accident benefits even if you were at fault. If fault is disputed, you may also be able to sue.

Q.04Do I have to go to the insurer's clinic?

No. You can choose where you get treatment. Pick a provider who has your interests in mind.

Q.05What should I put in the callback form?

Just your name, phone number and a sentence or two. Please don't send medical records or health details. We'll ask what we need when we call.
